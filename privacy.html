Hermes - Privacy Policy

Hermes is a personal application used by a single user, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Docs and Sheets) to read it and, only after the owner's explicit approval, to act on it.

Use and storage

Data is processed only to carry out the owner's requests. Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revocation

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
